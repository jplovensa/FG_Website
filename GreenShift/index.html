What does a first conversation cover?

Your goals, location, approximate size, existing information and desired timing. Photos or a plan are helpful. We confirm project fit and the next review; the online studio is a concept tool.

How are fees and delivery dates established?

They depend on the measured scope, site conditions, selected finishes and required coordination. Request a scoped proposal. The website does not provide a binding construction quote or an instant appointment.

Does every project take 45 days?

No. The rapid housing delivery system applies to eligible modular development projects after financing, design, approvals and site readiness are confirmed. Refurbishment and facade programmes are scoped individually.
